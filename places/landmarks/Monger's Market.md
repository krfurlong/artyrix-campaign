## Description
The under-dock region of [[Monger's Market]] is frequented most commonly by fishermen and day laborers who disembark in [[Estatione d'Este]] and immediately realize the city is an expensive place. A thin sheen of oily grease is ever present amid the rough cobblestone streets, and drydock repair shops populate much of the commerce of the area. Tritons down on their luck from [[Pythinia]] are commonly found working here, alongside various lizardfolk, kuo-toa, and sea elves. Commerce is done largely from tents and make-shift stalls that setup shop alongside the road: it's loud, its smelly, but the people here largely mean well - even if they've been at this grind for more years than they can recount.
