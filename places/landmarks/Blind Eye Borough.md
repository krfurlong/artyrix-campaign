## Description
A ragtag mess of twisting streets generally paved and repaved with concrete, flecks of brick and large stone characterize Blind Eye Borough. By some marvel of engineering or cruel trick of nature, light barely reaches even the larger thoroughfares of the neighborhood. Street signs are found everywhere and locals in heavy robes seem to consult them at every intersection, but the street names themselves are nearly indecipherable being crossed out and over-written time and time again. Mist lays low, giving a weighty-ness to the dim streets and cardboard-lined alleyways in which those that call the district home seem to camp, work, barter, pawn, and survive.
