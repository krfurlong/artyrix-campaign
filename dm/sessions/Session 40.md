## Intro
Time has a weird way of being anything but constant. Whether its that feeling of exhilaration that seems to accompany new sights and smells that makes each minute feel like a second. Or that funny way of moments slowing down to a crawl whenever you see a once-forgotten face for the first time in years. For Aoife, Kryst, and Kai, time manages to move slowly as you all collectively spring from your obscured alley to jump the goliath guard making their way towards the main metro stop of [[Blind Eye Borough]]...

For [[Bugsy]], however, you find yourself lost in a phase in which time ticks too quickly - as the flashes of spotty streetlights flash through the grimy windows of your and [[Sybil]]'s streetcar just now entering into the loose boundary of the Borough proper. That feeling, a premonition even, of being watched and seen by forces unbeknownst to you kicked your mind into overdrive, as you flicked through a mental rolodex of persons and groups that may be seeking your location and with the power to achieve their goal of finding you through arcane means. It's perhaps this fervor of mental activity that clouds your physical perception - even but for a moment - as [[Sybil]] you note, passively and curiously at first, but then with almost immediate alarm: the streaks of dusty light, while visible in the streetcars in front of you, seems to break like a wave upon water when nearing your metro's windows. Dark streaks of shadow begin to creep up the sides of the glass, like wisps of black veins stretching across skin pulled taught, before the shadows themselves coalesce in the middle of your metro-car, with golden yellow slits for eyes locked upon your duo as they begin to bear down the car towards you....

## Meeting [[Enzo Tzicara]]
re: [[Lost in Deep Cover]]

Enzo sulks down and throws a musty and sweat stained piece of fabric over his shoulders, seemingly becoming smaller and less descript in an instant. A moment later, he nearly darts from your sight as he slips into the shadows of [[Blind Eye Borough]] - twisting and turning around side streets and back alleys for nearly 40 minutes before he finally whips around to face you all and vet one last time that you were not being followed. Without saying a word, he reaches behind him - lifting a greasy piece of fabric draped over a jagged concrete wall - revealing a thin passage dimly lit by soft candlelight on the interior.

He knows:

- He was informed of the pullback from the larger force - but only after the fact and when he brought it up

- He's has a pre-scheduled meeting with his handler/a new contact in morning [[Shifty Dick's Shop Wares]]
