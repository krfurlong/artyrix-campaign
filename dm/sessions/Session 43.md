## Intro
As you hear the words "Book 'em!" echo through the stately marble interior, all of your eyes whip down to see a sharply dress and slightly smug looking halfling pointing up to the second floor chaos surrounding the entryway of the Records Room. A short moment later, you all are rushed by a group of maybe 20 guards - crowding your space and frisking you for weapons. You hear Dina del Pie yelling up "Careful with the harengon - he's a gassy one but he's got a fragile soul!" 

> Do you go peacefully?

Roll for BB - DC15 and lower --> she make a break for it and is peppered by bolts.

After some scuffling and disarming, you are led down the stairs and to the main entryway - and almost paraded in front of the figure you only presume to be [[Pierre O'Leary]]. **describe him** He nods at each of you - locking eyes with you [[Bugsy]] and doing a slight double take at [[Sybil]] before flicking his head as a wordless direction to the rest of the force. You are are led and thrown into the main level holding cells - where you had recently just been. Although this time the doors are locked shut and your belongings taken from you and stowed in the lockers in an adjacent room (booking & processing). As the guards leave your immediate area to return to the foyer, your left largely to yourselves for a moment for your soon to be questioners to collect themselves. 

Cutting through the almost silence that settled upon this scene, you hear a familiar gravely but distinguished voice of [[Leopold Harvey]]: *Get your hands off of me, Miles, by Moradin's hammer. I know you're following orders but I'm going peacefully and will not be led into what was once my **own** prison by any fellow officer - most especially you.*
