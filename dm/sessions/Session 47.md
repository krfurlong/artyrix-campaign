## Intro
Despite the vaulted ceiling of [[Taj Ferud]] and the lofty patchwork of stained glass windows all around you, the mood within the palace is best be described as oppressive. Air that should flow freely through the open windows instead weighs stagnant and heavy; light filtering is distorted and cast askew against the high walls - leaving the interior lit, but only dimly, and casting oddly prisonlike shadows upon the walls and floor. 

And yet - the ambiance shifted even darker as the large double doors of the central hall were pushed open; almost acting as a vacuum to suck and semblance of optimism out from the hallway and into an even more opulent yet uncomfortably sinister chamber:

Before you, you all step into a large domed hall - with tall pillar and lofty arches that like the hallways manage to both let light in while keeping the brightest of sun rays trapped in the upper echelons of the ceiling. On the opposite end hallway, you see a mess of plush sitting chairs, reclining ottomans, and throng of pillows in between two somewhat off-center ornate metal daises. A large table - perhaps 15x15 feet - stands in between you and the makeshift and odd throne.
