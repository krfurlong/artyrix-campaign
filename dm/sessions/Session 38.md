## Potential Leads

### Visiting [[Miles Lobo]]

- Transit on lines in The City Proper run 10gp/person - with transfers running an additional 5gp/line/person
	- Transfer costs are new - signs around the ticket station advertise:
	  "Join the Rosevale Transit Administration and help us to put your fares to work!"
	- The Mid-Violet line is completely new and popular for different reasons
		- Partitioned higher density crime districts onto their own line, improving safety for the others 
- Transit times:
	- Crossing the city by foot: ~6hrs per district
	- Crossing by light rail: ~2hrs per district
	- Ticket stations manned by [Machine Lieutenants](https://krfurlong.github.io/bestiary/creature/machine-lieutenant) brought in from Mechanus. They are incredibly inflexible and discern truth from lies by nature.  
- Transit narration:
	- As you take the Mid-Rogue line, you see the architecture fly by as you lurch forward out of the primary metro stop servicing [[Estatione d'Este]]. The lofty wrought metal and glass lined streets of the district gradually fade away as the stations tick by. In time, nearly 2 hours later you hear a gravely voice come out of nowhere: "Now entering: Parliamentary Square - Seat of governance for [[Île Follette]]." 
		- In contrast to the art deco coded [[Estatione d'Este]], the area surrounding the seat of power for the island was rather bland and functional: the streets here are intentionally wider, permitting more room for transit and the population to move through the true nexus of the city. As your tram rounds the corner, you all briefly glimpse the main governmental structure: [[The House of Open Doors]], a sleek and tall structure of clean white marble rising from an otherwise pristine and natural grassy knoll ([[Prismatic Park]]). A thin curtain of water trickles over each of the 4 sides of the structure, causing it to reflect the light of the sun and cast  flashes of color on the ground surrounding it.
	- Heading south now, that same voice echoes through the tram: "Now entering: [[Twilight Commons]]." The streets here quickly narrow from the near freeway-sized lanes of [[Parlimentary Square]] but somehow maintain an open and cozy feel about them. The buildings here are primarily brick and shorter than the high-rise structures in the surrounding districts, giving the neighborhood a sense of hominess and serenity. It's the sort of place that one could imagine living in, working in, and never leaving. The sort of place where city-folks of every stripe and color hope to raise a family in: Crime stays low, neighbors look out for one another, even the thrum of the larger city seems to melt away just a bit amid these sleepy streets.
