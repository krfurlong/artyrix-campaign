## Intro
As the aura vibes of [[Mother Marrow]]'s scry fade from her eyeball, you all are left in an eerie stillness amid the otherwise rowdy [[The Quadragauntlet]]: an apparent lull in the combat  coinciding with this moment of arcane reflection. But just for a moment - as all at once time rushes back into focus, pressing onward, as a loud roar erupts from the central ring - as Mother Marrow takes the opportunity to deft pop her eye back into its socket, and blinking thrice, giggles to herself - softly at first - but certainly present as she seems to relish the looks of distain and mild horror present across your faces; and lick her cracked lips while reflecting on the arcane visage just now fading from her fathomless pupil.

## Taking the Black-Line Cart from [[Pazuzu's Stables]]
The scene around [[Pazuzu's Stables]] is oddly very much the way you left it days ago - with the door still left ajar, smells of decay sit heavy amongst the streets and intensify upon nearing the apparently deserted warehouse.

In the basement, upon descending: a single candle is lit, illuminating the bright yellow eyes of what's most certainly a tabaxi - and a large one at that.

> Oh dear inspector - you thought you could simply escape the city so easily, hm? I have been expecting you - no hunting you this entire time. A fugitive from the state, I can most certainly understand by the way [[Pierre O'Leary]] seemed to have treated you...but a fugitive from your debts: where is the honor and dignity in that, my friend. Let us be gentlemen now and speak plainly - you owe me - [[Binjar the Flecked]] - and my associates quite a bit of coin. And we intend to collect on it presently.
> 
> Should you refuse, and I sincerely hope you do not, things will get messy, blood will be spilled, and your coin will still leave here in my purse. 
> Cooperate with us and you may find that many in [[The Stallignment]] can be quite friendly, myself included - all for the onetime low price of 1600gp (up from 1150; or 1500 earlier upon arrival; adding in near daily surcharge fees)
