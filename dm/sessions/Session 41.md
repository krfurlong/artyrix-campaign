## Breaking into [[Pazuzu's Stables]]
### Description
Maybe at one time this warehouse was used to house actual beasts of burden, but it's clear that it's been retrofitted time and time over to accommodate much darker deeds.

3 wide aisles stretch maybe 50 feet down before ending in a wall marked only by a set large sliding doors, much like the front of the structure. 

The innermost cells are smaller, and divided by 15 ft tall panels of frosty opaque glass making it nearly impossible to discern the individual beforehand. 

The outermost cells are larger - sometimes holding individuals who would otherwise exceed the limitations of the small interior cells. The opaque glass was substituted for reinforced, narrow iron bars - allowing surveillance at all times.

A faint hum of something electric hangs in the air, though you are unsure as to its source. The cells themselves are separated by iron and twisting brass pipes, seemingly made to circulate the virulent air around the facility. Finally, a single red glyph, apparently carved through the stone floor and into the ground itself, radiates with a dark and sickening thrum of foreboding, seemingly dimming the torches around the room with every passing pulse.

Back room: As you press the button, a loud "clunk" cuts through the air as the metallic floor to your left begins to slowly descend as a type of elevator...
Do you get on?
Perhaps 30 seconds later, the elevator stops - ending at a small 20x20 foot platform adjacent to a thin rail track that immediately enters a tunnel on either side of the end of the platform. You are in near darkness, the only light coming dimly from elevator chute - A single unlit lantern hangs on a nearby post, the only material thing currently down here.

NB: every ~20 minutes, a mine car runs down, piloted by a black kobold on the payroll of [[The Stallignment]]. They don't ask questions and aren't paid for providing intel, they do a service and they're proud of their jobs and their craftsmanship. They stop their carts if the lantern is lit - otherwise, they continue on without stopping.
