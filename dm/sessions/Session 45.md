## Intro
Alongside the dusty outlaying roads of [[New Sigil]], a single bird of prey soars across the horizon in front of the early morning sun - casting a long shadow over a scene of utter carnage. The bodies of three duergar laymen lay scattered, beaten and bloodied - leaving a trail of viscera to the sole figure alive from [[The Kismet]]'s entourage: Myrnura Brokenhold. The large wagon rests on its side, wheel broken thrice over, and the smell of decay cuts through the air like bleach; the two Deep Rothe' nowhere to be seen.
 
As time steps back into pace - you fully take stock of your surroundings: a group of carts crests your sightline heading north towards your group perhaps an hour or two off; A wooden appearing fort which you now know as the checkpoint [[Southern Pylon]] perhaps a 2-3 hour trek ahead of you. The rush of the past 24+ hours of being awake begin to gnaw on the sides of your vision as you fight of a wave of weariness to face the morning sun and new day.
