
- **Dust of Sneezing and Choking (Uncommon):** A deceptive powder that appears to be _Dust of Disappearance_. When thrown, it forces creatures in a 10-foot radius to succeed on a DC 15 Constitution save or become incapacitated and suffocated by uncontrollable sneezing fits.
	- 3 uses. 100gp/pinch
    
- **Oil of Taggit (Poison, Rare):** A contact poison often smeared on doorknobs or the inside of gloves. A creature subjected to this poison must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 24 hours. The poisoned creature is unconscious.
	- 400gp
    
- **Midnight Tears (Poison, Rare):** An ingestible poison for the patient killer. A creature that ingests this poison suffers no effect until the stroke of midnight. If the poison has not been neutralized before then, the victim must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 31 (9d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
	- 750gp
    
- **Potion of Gaseous Form (Rare):** Marketed as the "Cell-Breaker's Brew." It allows a thief to turn into a misty cloud, slipping through keyholes, under doors, or through sewer grates to bypass any physical lock or guard.
	- 250gp
    
- **Philter of Love (Uncommon):** In a criminal den, this isn't for romance—it’s for social engineering. It makes the victim "charmed" by the first person they see for one hour. It’s the perfect tool for getting a guard to look the other way or a noble to sign a disastrous contract.
	- 100gp
    
- **The "Blackjack" (Non-Magical Weighted Sap):** A specialized martial weapon (Club) used for silent takedowns.
    
    - _Unique Use:_ If you hit a creature that is surprised with this weapon, you can choose to deal no damage. If you do, the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 + your Dex modifier + Proficiency) or be stunned until the end of its next turn.
    - 300gp
        
        
- **The Garrote Wire (Non-Magical Martial Weapon):** A thin, reinforced wire with handles at both ends, favored by the syndicate’s "cleaners."
    
    - _Unique Use:_ While you have Advantage on an attack roll against a humanoid, you can attempt to grapple them with the garrote. On a success, the target is restrained and cannot speak or cast spells with verbal components until the grapple ends.
    - Target takes 1d6 damage per turn as an action
    - 400gp
        
- **Potion of Diminution (Rare):** For when you need to hide in a place no one would think to look. Shrinking to "Tiny" size provides a +5 bonus to Stealth checks and allows a spy to hide inside a vase, a desk drawer, or even a pocket.
	- 400gp
