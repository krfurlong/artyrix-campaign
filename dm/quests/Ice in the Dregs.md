## Setup
- Izzy is indeed freezing the pipes but she's a whistleblower/former employee
	- She discovered a massive fire elemental is powering the entire system in [[The Deep Dregs]] and upwards.
	- When she discovered it, it was nearing breaking from its chains
	- Burm was not prepared to deal with the situation, doesn't have the funds to cover a re-sealing but had insurance on the system from a group of businesses in [[Ashworks District]] and is thus hoping to collect on that when the system breaks
- Izzy is freezing the pipes to keep the elemental at bay for longer - 
- She set traps in the pipelines to deter people from going after her, they lower people's speed by 10ft for 1 min (DC14 Con save) and 1d6 damage; DC16 to disarm
