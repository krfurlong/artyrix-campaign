#lore 

## The 8 Path Outcomes & Boons

### 1. Solitary $\rightarrow$ Diplomacy $\rightarrow$ Respect

**Path Profile:** _The Quiet Hermit & Saintly Healer_

You sought solitude, de-escalated conflict without violence, and honored the fallen. You embody Eldath as the silent, compassionate guardian of sacred places.

- **Boon: Grace of the Quiet Grove**
    
    - **Passive:** You gain proficiency in Religion (or expertise if already proficient). Additionally, non-hostile beast creatures will never attack you unless provoked.
        
    - **Active (1/Long Rest):** As an action, you radiate an aura of absolute serenity in a 15-foot radius for 1 minute. Hostile creatures that enter or start their turn in the aura must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (against your Spell Save DC) or be affected by _Calm Emotions_ until the aura ends or they take damage.
        

### 2. Solitary $\rightarrow$ Diplomacy $\rightarrow$ Neglect

**Path Profile:** _The Detached Ascetic_

You walked alone and chose peace, but viewed the physical corpse as merely a discarded vessel. You embody Eldath’s focus on the transcendent spirit over worldly remains.

- **Boon: Veil of the Mist Veil**
    
    - **Passive:** You learn the _Mending_ and _Spare the Dying_ cantrips. They do not count against your total cantrips known.
        
    - **Active (1/Long Rest):** When you cast a spell that restores hit points, you can choose to become invisible until the end of your next turn or until you make an attack or cast another spell.
        

### 3. Solitary $\rightarrow$ Violence $\rightarrow$ Respect

**Path Profile:** _The Reluctant Sentinel_

You preferred to be left alone, but when forced into conflict, you resorted to steel—yet maintained solemn reverence for the life you were forced to end. You embody Eldath as a protector who knows the tragic weight of necessary force.

- **Boon: Weight of the Fallen Leaf**
    
    - **Passive:** When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points using non-lethal damage, you regain temporary hit points equal to your Paladin level + your Charisma modifier.
        
    - **Active (1/Long Rest):** When you land a melee weapon attack, you can channel divine regret. Instead of dealing normal Smite damage, the target takes no damage but is instantly subjected to the _Hold Person_ or _Hold Monster_ spell (no spell slot required, save against your Spell Save DC).
        

### 4. Solitary $\rightarrow$ Violence $\rightarrow$ Neglect

**Path Profile:** _The Primal Solitary_

You sought isolation, struck down those who intruded upon your peace, and left them where they fell. You embody the harsh, unyielding isolation of wild nature where nature reclaims all things quickly.

- **Boon: Ward of the Unbroken Wild**
    
    - **Passive:** Your movement speed increases by 10 feet while you are not wearing heavy armor, and you cannot be tracked by non-magical means.
        
    - **Active (1/Short or Long Rest):** As a reaction when hit by an attack, you can cause thorned vines and rushing water to manifest. You gain a +5 bonus to AC against that attack (like the _Shield_ spell), and the attacker must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet back and knocked prone.
        

### 5. Commoners $\rightarrow$ Diplomacy $\rightarrow$ Respect

**Path Profile:** _The Paragon Peacemaker_

You walked with the people, de-escalated tension peacefully, and treated the tragic death with full holy honors. You represent the idealized Eldathyn mediator and pillar of the community.

- **Boon: Blessing of the Still Waters**
    
    - **Passive:** You gain proficiency in Persuasion (or expertise if already proficient). Your _Lay on Hands_ pool increases by an amount equal to twice your Paladin level.
        
    - **Active (1/Long Rest):** As an action, you touch a creature and cleanse them of all physical and mental burdens. This removes one level of exhaustion and ends any conditions currently affecting them from the following list: Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, Poisoned, Stunned, or Frightened.
        

### 6. Commoners $\rightarrow$ Diplomacy $\rightarrow$ Neglect

**Path Profile:** _The Practical Shepherd_

You stayed with the crowd and negotiated peace, but focused your energy entirely on comforting the living rather than lingering on the dead. You embody Eldath’s focus on preserving life in the present moment.

- **Boon: Mantle of the Living Sanctuary**
    
    - **Passive:** Whenever you use _Lay on Hands_ on an ally, both you and the target gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage until the start of your next turn.
        
    - **Active (1/Long Rest):** When an ally within 30 feet of you drops to 0 hit points but isn’t killed outright, you can use your reaction to cause them to drop to 1 hit point instead, accompanied by a soothing burst of mist that pushes all enemies within 5 feet of them 10 feet away.
        

### 7. Commoners $\rightarrow$ Violence $\rightarrow$ Respect

**Path Profile:** _The Martyred Shield_

You walked among the strangers and fought to defend them, but mourned the necessity of death by honoring the fallen foe. You embody Eldath as a shield for the weak who grieves every life lost.

- **Boon: Sorrow of the Cascading Falls**
    
    - **Passive:** While carrying a shield, allies standing within 5 feet of you gain a +2 bonus to their Armor Class and Dexterity saving throws.
        
    - **Active (1/Short or Long Rest):** When you deal Divine Smite damage to a target, you can choose to transform that radiant energy into a soothing wave. Up to three creatures of your choice within 30 feet regain hit points equal to half the Smite damage dealt.
        

### 8. Commoners $\rightarrow$ Violence $\rightarrow$ Neglect

**Path Profile:** _The Zealous Vigilante_

You chose the crowd, eliminated the threat with swift violence, and moved on without looking back. You took the most pragmatic path, straying furthest from Eldath’s core tenets of pacifism and quiet reflection.

- **Boon: Lesson of the Stagnant Pool** _(A "Flawed" or Complex Blessing)_
    
    - **Passive:** You gain a +2 bonus to initiative rolls, but you have disadvantage on saving throws against being Charmed, as your mind lacks the stillness required for Eldath’s deepest protections.
        
    - **Active (1/Long Rest):** As a bonus action, you can coat your weapon in murky, stagnant divine energy for 1 minute. On a hit, the target takes an extra $1\text{d}8$ Poison or Radiant damage (your choice) and must succeed on a Constitution save or be _Poisoned_ until the end of their next turn.
