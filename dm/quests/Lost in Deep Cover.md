## Premise
[[Johnboy Scivone]] - [[New Sigil Police Department (NSPD)]]'s *Chief Deployment Officer* was brought in under [[Pierre O'Leary]] to lead a "Transparency Modernization" campaign.

### Stated Goal
- Improve relationships with all New Sigil Boroughs through clear documentation of policing practices
- Single point of communication for complaints
- Directly accountable to [[Pierre O'Leary]]

### Underlying Motives
- [[Pierre O'Leary]] was deeply uncomfortable with a slate of missions that previously only reported to [[Leopold Harvey]] - codenamed: **[[SkunkOps]]**
	- POCs were recruited specifically with ties to [[The Kismet]], [[The Stallignment]], and [[The Ascendent]] to work as undercover
	- **SkunkOps** worked under deep cover - only meeting with single POCs as one-offs and notoriously had no contact between them or awareness whether they were among friends or not.
	- They're report out critical intel back to [[New Sigil Police Department (NSPD)]] at sporadic cadences, which proved essential in some major busts
- [[Pierre O'Leary]] directed [[Johnboy Scivone]] to "break ties" with all members of the **SkunkOps**, due to a fear of their retribution against NSPD (e.g., coming clean) - which would constitute a massive scandal and threaten the [[Tethis Rosevale]] administration
	- [[Johnboy Scivone]] went one step further by systematically orchestrating the murder of all SkunkOps members - "cleaning house sometimes breaks some plates"
	- Generally aiding relations between [[The Kismet]] and [[The Stallignment]] with the Rosevale administration, with little to no publicity outside of [[Blind Eye Borough]]
