## His vibe
Pierre presents as a morally grey figure, heavily inspired by the film-noir theme. His eyes are hard set and difficult to read, through the nicks and scars along his hands and cheeks would suggest his life hasn't always been easy. He carries himself now with a swagger that hides any indecision from his past, and commands a presence in all rooms, despite his halfling stature.

Pierre is a halfling and chief of [[New Sigil]]'s large metropolis's police department. He wears 1920's style grab, including suspenders and a fedora, and pinstripe blue/grey pants. No jacket, but he does roll up his sleeves, which shows off hairy arms. He wears a thin black tie but has the top button of his shirt undone. A billy club, small leather-bound journal and pistol are holstered around his belt.
